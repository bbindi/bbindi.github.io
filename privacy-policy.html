Privacy Policy for Tsk.R: Procrastinateless

Effective Date: October 14, 2026
Developer: Brixavier (com.brixavier.tskr)

1. Overview

Tsk.R: Procrastinateless (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App. Tsk.R is designed as an offline-first productivity application, meaning your personal task, plan, and note data remains strictly local to your device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • Local Task & Productivity Data: All task titles, due dates, notes, plan steps, and checklist items created within Tsk.R are stored locally on your device using a private local SQLite/Room database in application-private storage. We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal task or plan content on external cloud servers.
  • No Personal Account Data: We do not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

3. Permissions Used

To provide core functionality and advertising integration, Tsk.R requests the following Android system permissions:

  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to serve Google AdMob advertisements and manage User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy consent choices.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger time-sensitive task reminders and Mission Mode notification alerts precisely when due.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display upcoming task warnings and countdown alerts on Android 13+.
  • Vibration & Audio (VIBRATE / WAKE_LOCK / DISABLE_KEYGUARD / USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required to trigger haptic feedback, keep screen active during alarm events, and launch full-screen Mission Mode alerts when due.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & UMP SDK: Tsk.R incorporates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to serve advertisements and manage user privacy choices.
  • Data Safety & Identifiers: Google processes non-personal device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and performance diagnostics for ad serving, frequency capping, and fraud prevention as governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Your personal tasks, plans, and notes are never shared with or accessible to AdMob or any third party.
  • Google Play Services (In-App Updates & Reviews): Tsk.R interacts with official Google Play Services APIs to check for application updates and facilitate optional Google Play Store reviews. No personal task content, notes, or review text is collected, stored, or processed by Tsk.R during these interactions.
  • Privacy Choices: Users in applicable regions (such as the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland) can manage or revoke their advertising privacy consent at any time via Settings → Privacy Choices within the App.

5. Children’s Privacy

Tsk.R does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general productivity tool intended for users aged 13 and older.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective Date” at the top of this policy.
